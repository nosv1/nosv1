# Big employers within ~50 miles of Kansas City, MO

He asked (2026-11-17, driving home from Wichita) for the region's biggest companies, so he can pick one at random
and look for a seat inside that fits. His target, from the same day: **hands-on operations work with data access and
room to automate his own job** (logistics, inventory control, operations or transportation analyst), day shift, no
customer-facing or KPI-owner roles, no car auctions or car pricing. Honeywell is OK.

**This is not a ranked list.** Ingram's ranks the region's companies every year, but it blocks automated access.
The names are from Claude's knowledge, checked against the sources at the bottom. Headcounts and ownership change,
so check a company's own careers page before acting.

★ = has hands-on operations, logistics or inventory work on site, closest to his target.

## How to use it

"coordinator." Keep only day-shift roles in the metro. If a seat looks like his dealership job with more data, that's
the one.
